Common questions

Answers you'd actually find useful.

Mortgage questions, answered the way Katie would answer them in person.

How do I get pre-approved for a mortgage in BC?

Start with a short call or the online application. Katie reviews your income, down payment, and goals, then checks lender fit to get you a pre-approval letter you can actually use.

Can self-employed borrowers qualify for a mortgage?

Yes — it happens more than you'd think. Katie works with lenders who accept tax returns, bank statements, and alternative income documentation.

How long does mortgage approval take on Vancouver Island?

Simple pre-approvals can come back same day. Full approvals depend on the property, appraisal, and documentation. Katie gives you a realistic timeline upfront.

What's the difference between a broker and a bank?

A broker like Katie works with dozens of lenders to find the best fit. A bank can only offer their own products. Broker services are typically free to the borrower.

When should I start the mortgage process?

Earlier than you think. A pre-approval call before house hunting lets you know your real budget and strengthens your offers. Book a call — it takes 15 minutes.

Does Katie work with buyers in Alberta too?

Yes — Katie is licensed in both BC and Alberta. Whether you're buying on Vancouver Island, in Metro Vancouver, or in Alberta, she can help.
